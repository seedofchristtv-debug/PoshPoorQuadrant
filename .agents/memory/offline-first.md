---
name: Ministry offline use
description: The ministry app must support field use without a reliable network and synchronize saved work later.
---

The app is expected to remain useful during internet outages, especially for member registration with phone photos and Sunday attendance. Save entries on the device while offline and sync them when connectivity returns; explain that first-time caching and device/browser storage retention are prerequisites and limits.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires offline usability because ministry work may happen without reliable connectivity.

**How to apply:** Preserve offline registration, camera/photo capture where supported, attendance entry, durable local queuing, and later sync in future app changes. Never describe browser-based offline storage as guaranteed against clearing site data or device loss.
